\section{Experience}
\entry{Graduate Research Assistant}{CVAR Lab (Computer Vision and Robotics), University of Maine}{2025 -- present}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Benchmarked Meta's SAM 3 and SAM 3.1 on KITTI-MOTS and MOTS20 with the official HOTA evaluation on cloud A100 GPUs. SAM 3 scored
        82.6 car HOTA (val) and kept identity through 94\% of 245 real occlusions; SAM 3.1 scored 74.6 and 63\%.
  \item Traced about half of SAM 3.1's deficit to its shipped tracking settings by reading Meta's source code; a controlled rerun raised
        its car HOTA from 74.0 to 78.3. Isolated a separate fault that halts it on long, crowded sequences.
  \item Built a Kalman-filter identity layer on SAM's output that returns a lost ID when the object reappears, raising SAM 3.1's identity
        kept through occlusion from 63\% to 70\% (p = 0.004).
  \item Built a pipeline that mined 10,871 pedestrian occlusions from 1,256 NVIDIA driving clips in 12 countries, using 3D ray casting
        against labelled boxes and lidar depth projected into a fisheye camera.
  \item Showed on that real street geometry that an occlusion-aware tracker keeps identity through 1 to 5 second hides in 57\% of
        1,600 cases, where a standard tracker keeps 2\%; driven by a real YOLOv8 detector, 44\% against 3\%.
  \item Built mobile robots end to end, from sensors and wiring to ROS 2 nodes; implemented EKF localization fusing IMU, wheel odometry
        and lidar, with costmap planning and obstacle avoidance.
\end{itemize}
